Before scoring I opened eight actual same-host input images: B01 G001/G018/G034, B02/B03/B04/B05 middle G images, and B01 AP001. Eight visually opened images are not an exhaustive manual review of 233 unique media.

After scoring I opened six more decisive G tiles: B01 G006/G009 for its **wrong** G+AP continuation, B03 G014/G017 and B04 G014/G015 for the **wrong** G-SEQ repeat decisions. Their cited local tokens exist on the actual gray masks, but neighboring fish, skipped sampled times and close crossings prevent those two-frame views from certifying the model's asserted continuous identity. This is a cautious post-answer observation, not proof the complete G sequence lacked enough information. The B01 AP failure cannot be attributed solely to color because AP also changes visual localization context.

The independent programmatic [citation audit](VISUAL_CITATION_AUDIT.jsonl) opened the **actual bytes sent** for all 106 decisive citations of ten non-abstaining outputs, checked SHA-256, grayscale pixels, normalized mask geometry and token/time source: 106/106 passed those source/pixel checks. This is weaker than semantic validation. The model's descriptions of motion, occlusion continuity, role persistence and alternative identity remain `UNKNOWN_NOT_ESTABLISHED_BY_SOURCE_CHECK` for all 106 unless separately adjudicated; no model self-declared cue was accepted as ground truth. In particular, B01 G+AP describes a “continuous” route through crowded frames, while B03/B04 G-SEQ repeats infer pairings from relative location despite acknowledged gaps. Their cited pixels are real; the inferred cross-time identities are wrong against the already exposed event key.
