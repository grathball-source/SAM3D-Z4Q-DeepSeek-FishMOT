# R0 and E1 plan, fixed before E1 API

Research hypothesis: given identical causal history, legal complete candidates and scoring events, temporal image plus text evidence lets `deepseek-flash` rank identity associations better than active numerical D/A/M ranking. This is a new hypothesis. E1 is offline shadow selection only.

R0 uses the sealed 2888-frame validation replay, checks request-byte and response-hash binding, and tests repaired lineage, epoch and relation scoring in isolated source copies. No historical prompt, three-vote gate, five-frame confirmation or episode budget is changed.

E1 event registration uses two current masks marked as neighbors. One event per public-identity pair per six seconds prevents contact flicker from multiplying samples. A query occurs after both B0 owners are observable, no longer neighbors, and 15 further acquired frames have arrived within six seconds. At most one query per event. Three clean causal references per history identity are selected at least five frames apart when available; missing references stay missing. Current segments use query-14, query-7 and query frames if that native mask exists. Actual timestamps are retained.

The scope is at most two historical identities and three current observations. B0 owners are included, plus current native masks newly seen after contact within the registered contact radius. Larger components are OUT_OF_SCOPE. Candidates are complete pair swaps of selected observations, with all other current IDs held fixed. Every candidate is checked on a cloned old Z4Q stage; only executable alternatives enter the model packet. Events lacking one are retained in the audit and receive no request. There is no GT-based candidate addition.

Eligible events are sorted by SHA-256 of the registered episode ID. The first 12 from development and first 12 from exposed validation are frozen. All five arms share each packet, cutoff and candidate set: B0, active numerical N, L-T, L-V-static and L-V-temporal. Repeat and text-alias permutation are diagnostics only. The permutation keeps image bytes and row order unchanged. E1 uses a new relative preference output contract, with deterministic strict-winner decoding; ties, cycles and unobservable comparisons fall back to B0.

Predictions and model decisions must be sealed before a separate scoring process may open GT. Offline event correctness is not IDF1/HOTA. No automatic E2 follows E1.
