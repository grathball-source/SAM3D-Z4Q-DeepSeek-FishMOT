The first frozen case `AO0-V-GT2-GT6-1322` is one physical interaction, despite two GT identities, repeated contact-run fragments and more than 1,200 subsequent changed relationships per individual. `validation_rel_v2/RELATION_SUMMARY.json` and `RELATION_SEGMENTS.json` show GT2 public 1→4 after a 15-frame no-unique interval, and GT6 public 4→1 at frame 1359. Earlier pre-contact frame 1300 uniquely links the two GT objects to 1 and 4. This anchor is retrospective exposed-GT diagnostic, not a numerical/public-name identity prior for a model.

Prediction-only `validation/CONTACT_PROBE_WIDE.json` registers raw 1/4 contact runs from 1322 to 1419, with at most two missing-contact frames between runs. Its first qualifying 15-frame stable, quality-passing, contact-free query is 1484–1498, 79 frames after contact end. The initial `validation/CONTACT_PROBE.json` clipped the scan at 1380 and misreported the contact end. It is retained as a failed preparatory diagnostic and was **not** the frozen query source; the wide probe SHA is in `CASE_MANIFEST.json`. No query was chosen by post-query GT reward.

At 1498 the complete KEEP and SWAP mappings use current native 1/4 and leave every other native/mask unchanged. `STATE_ACTION.json` confirms quality, past anchors, no contact, unique occupancy, no qualified native-return priority, and an accepted real `Bridge.stage`. `COUNTERFACTUAL_SEAL.json` fixes the oracle and independent state output before GT scoring. The stage's inherited provenance string `DEEPSEEK_CLOSED_LOOP` is code metadata only; no model call occurred.

The predeclared early validation GT1 change 2→6→2 (126–279) is a separate possible relation event, but prediction-only `validation/SECOND_PROBE_2_6.json` found contact 126–189 and no qualifying two-owner 15/150 query. The older E1C frame-205 missing-ID proposals targeted native 5 or 1, not a confirmed GT1 current native, so they are not silently adopted as an AO0 correction. A different single-missing-ID candidate protocol was not frozen for this second case; this is **not** a claim that no recovery could ever exist. Development GT1's 0→6→0 interval has no registered 0/6 predicted pair contact in `development/SECOND_PROBE_0_6.json`. Validation GT5 5→11→5 lasts 26 unique-match frames near the split end; development GT3 2→8→2 lasts 19. None is promoted by relaxed follow-up or retrospective oracle selection. The independently qualifying second sustained case count is **zero** in this bounded AO0 set.

The four old E1 model-harm packet IDs in `CASES.jsonl` and `experiments/rq0_memory_headroom/PRIOR_FAILURE_BINDINGS.md` remain negative controls. Their old responses, images and scores were not rerun or modified.
