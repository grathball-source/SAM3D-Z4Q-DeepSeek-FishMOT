# Actual-pixel QA boundary

Work was already exposed to AO0/M1/M2-T answers. This is **post-score QA, not an independent blind identity judgment**. Pixels and raw GT raster are not committed.

`DECISIVE_PIXEL_SCAN.jsonl` records actual byte/SHA verification and decoding of **all 185** decisive directional citations in the ten non-abstaining outputs: 106 SUPPORT and 79 CONTRADICT, over 79 distinct attempt/image pairs. Every cited G image decoded as grayscale, and each cited token bounding region contained nonwhite pixels (zero blank regions). This establishes byte/source and local geometry presence, **not** whether two cited objects are the same fish. The older 106-citation check only covered selected SUPPORT; this audit adds competing CONTRADICT references.

I separately opened **11 actually sent G tiles** at original tile resolution: B01 G009/G011/G014/G018/G025/G032, B02 G014, B03 G014/G017, B04 G014/G015. I also opened B01 `AP001`, the actually sent endpoint RGB crop. All are SHA-checked against `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`; their complete local restricted paths, byte counts and SHA-256 values are in `ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.json`. B01 G014 has touching/crowded mask silhouettes, and G018/G025 show several nearby local objects; the local `f###:o##` labels clearly restart per frame. These views support the need for a chain audit but alone do not certify identity across skipped times. B03/B04 paired endpoint views also do not establish an uninterrupted track. B01 AP001 contains more than one visible fish; its role label is not a pixel-isolated single-fish identity proof.

I additionally opened five original B01 RGB frames (1331, 1353, 1384, **1400**, 1437). Every one is `AUDIT_ONLY_NOT_SENT_RGB`: the model received G masks at 1331/1353/1384/1437, not those raw RGB frames; frame 1400 had **no sent G image either**. Original RGB displays the real crowded tank, but cannot be used to claim the frozen model saw color or frame 1400. These images are also inventoried off Git. No fabricated fish, interpolation, or GT overlay was used.

The posthoc GT-to-native comparisons in `FIRST_DIVERGENCE.jsonl` are a separate table from model inputs. B01 A-Y first wrong G+AP links a GT6-certified cited step at 1331 to a GT2-cited step at 1437; the repeat B-X links GT2 at 1331 to GT6 at 1384. The interval is the first **certifiable mismatch among explicit cited steps**, not a visual determination of the exact physical switch frame. Ambiguous/mixed match observations remain UNKNOWN. Manual views do not upgrade uninspected frames to independent semantic verification.
