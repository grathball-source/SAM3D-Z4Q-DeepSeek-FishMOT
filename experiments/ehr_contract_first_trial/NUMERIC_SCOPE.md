# Numerical information boundary

All original numerical comparator outputs are retained by fixed SHA from the previous source run. N-H2D and N-HD use endpoint history rather than the full anonymous event history supplied to H-2D/H-D. They are descriptive controls, not a full same-information comparator. This run does not substitute two-frame IoU for history, tune a new threshold, select a new anchor, or claim VLM necessity from an unequal comparison.

Named position, velocity, depth, and quality derive from the qualified original observation/depth streams. B01-A velocity and B05-Y velocity remain `UNKNOWN`; no cross-risk velocity is filled. Anonymous event depth/quality remains source measured and does not certify identity. The new E condition intentionally removes derived history measurements; all H conditions keep their original complete source measurements, with H-2D versus H-D differing by the original depth channel only.
