## SLR-2: text versus text + image, offline

Authoritative report: [RESULTS.md](offline/slr2_text_vs_image/RESULTS.md), with [REVIEW.md](offline/slr2_text_vs_image/REVIEW.md), [SUMMARY.json](offline/slr2_text_vs_image/SUMMARY.json), [EVENT_RESULTS.json](offline/slr2_text_vs_image/EVENT_RESULTS.json), and [MODEL_DECISIONS.json](offline/slr2_text_vs_image/MODEL_DECISIONS.json). ## Closed loop: real API, independent tracking state

Authoritative server report: [RESULTS.md](online/closed_loop_2888/RESULTS.md). The run used global frames 9301–12188 (local 1–2888) of an exposed validation segment. [SOURCE_FREEZE.json](online/closed_loop_2888/SOURCE_FREEZE.json) fixes source, config, and input hashes. [PREFLIGHT_V2.json](online/closed_loop_2888/PREFLIGHT_V2.json) records exact B0 replay, [CONTRACT_CHECKS_V2.json](online/closed_loop_2888/CONTRACT_CHECKS_V2.json) the engineering fixtures, and [SCORER_FIXTURE.json](online/closed_loop_2888/SCORER_FIXTURE.json) a scorer counterexample. The latter file is the original mixed diagnostic preamble plus JSON, preserved byte-for-byte despite its `.json` suffix.

Three independent DeepSeek votes were required per eligible check, followed by the original five-frame confirmation, three-check episode cap, stage, and atomic commit. There were 11 checks in five episode keys and 33 real responses. The gate never selected a modification; B1 committed zero transactions. [CALL_LEDGER.jsonl](online/closed_loop_2888/CALL_LEDGER.jsonl), `requests/`, and `responses/` hold the text-only call evidence. The prediction and transaction streams were sealed before GT scoring; see [PREDICTIONS_SEALED.json](online/closed_loop_2888/PREDICTIONS_SEALED.json), [METRICS.json](online/closed_loop_2888/METRICS.json), [POSTSEAL_EVENT_AUDIT.json](online/closed_loop_2888/POSTSEAL_EVENT_AUDIT.json), and [FULL_TIMELINE_HARM_AUDIT.json](online/closed_loop_2888/FULL_TIMELINE_HARM_AUDIT.json). `predictions_validation.jsonl.gz` and `transactions_validation.jsonl.gz` preserve the actual replay outputs.

The critical cases are documented in the online report: frame 190's incorrect but reachable 5→2 claim was deferred; 241/246 had a locally matching 7→2 candidate but remained deferred; frame 251 disagreed across votes; late 2832–2849 alternatives were also deferred. These observations explain why there was no update. They do not show a counterfactual gain from any rejected proposal. B0 and B1 had identical IDF1 80.6975868362, HOTA 69.2438689597, AssA 60.0743214554, IDSW 9, FP 298 and FN 423; see the sealed metrics for full precision.

The online prompt and agreement gate are a **different protocol** from SLR-2. Comparing 17/18 offline to zero online commits is informative about translation from a local selection task to a guarded tracker, but is not a controlled single-variable ablation of model quality, input representation, and timing.
