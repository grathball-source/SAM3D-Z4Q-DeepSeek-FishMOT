## Hypothesis and opportunity boundary

The old group-q controller misses identity breaks where a source disappears and a new native first appears outside a confirmed 2→1→2 group. A legal old reference plus a current measurement may support restoring that old public ID before the new source's first publication. No retry at the first *eligible* future frame. Initial segment identities and returning old native handles are recorded but not births.

Preserve that strict census. The formal endpoint contract instead explicitly retains the most recent jointly clean geometry/raw fragment before risk, not an implicit stale last value. It gives only a source-level upper bound of four raw and three retained-v2 eligible birth moments; branch versions, bank anchors, reservations and aliases can exclude them. No GT or scores informed this amendment. Do not mistake zero exposure for evidence that depth carries no identity information.

## Frozen history and physical interpretation

Each branch collects committed facts into a jointly clean same-generation/public/epoch contiguous fragment, at most 30 frames. At most ten actual observations enter each forecast. Qualifying raw core, area≥64, existing quality predicate, SOURCE_OBSERVATION class and no neighbors are required. Source gaps and version changes erase the reference. Any risk clears the live fit; the last completed fragment survives explicitly. A newly clean run replaces it even if only one or two points; never search backward for a better fragment. At disappearance freeze the exact fragment, reference endpoint, mechanical bank anchor/version and all subsequent risk facts.

The physical scoring reference is the actual last jointly clean observation, not necessarily the bank's earlier anchor. The actual bank anchor must independently resolve to its previously registered source/public/generation/epoch; integer public equality alone is insufficient. Risk observations remain anonymous, do not certify continuity, and are not joined into geometry or depth fits. Complete query-minus-reference time, including contact and missing frames, enters uncertainty and the unchanged 12-second limit. No interpolated path or physical velocity is invented.
