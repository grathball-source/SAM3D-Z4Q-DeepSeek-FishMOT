# AO0 frozen diagnostic protocol

This is an exposed-GT diagnostic, not a VLM effectiveness study or a new tracker. Fixed review base: `6d090f9`. The old RQ0/E1/E1C outputs and official metrics stay read-only. No model/API call, new training, test GT, SAM3 internal-memory edit, or quality-threshold search is permitted. User authorization in the AO0 attachment supersedes the older branch-only/main prohibition for this delivery; it does not rewrite that older history.

1. Build a complete exposed development/validation GT–native–predicted-mask–public-ID timeline. The co-occurrence matrix and public changes are descriptive. Absolute public numbers have no intrinsic GT meaning. A unique prediction requires the existing ambiguity-filtered `objects.gt_id` match. Missing or mixed masks remain `NO_UNIQUE_OBSERVATION`.
2. Select at most two sustained relation-change cases by exposed sequence evidence, prioritizing validation GT2/GT6. The case event may be GT-enriched; query time may not. Freeze `CASE_MANIFEST.json` and `CASES.jsonl` before the counterfactual. Four old E1 harmful packet IDs remain negative controls.
3. For the first case, use predicted pair-contact records only. The registered contact episode consists of the raw pair-contact runs from 1322 through 1419 (intervening gaps of at most two frames). The first query after the episode must have both complete current observations, unchanged native/epoch and original engine quality, no predicted contact, for 15 consecutive acquired frames; it must fall within 150 frames of the episode end. No later/lower-GT-error query is permitted.
4. At the frozen query, compare complete `KEEP` and `SWAP` mappings. The output-only oracle swaps public IDs 1 and 4 on every mask from query through split end, including any later native or alias producing those public IDs; all other IDs and masks remain untouched. It never changes pre-query output, never chooses labels frame-by-frame from GT, and does not alter engine state. Score both complete splits with archived TrackEval and all-object follow-up.
5. Independently replay the same pre-query history through the existing `Bridge.preview`, `stage`, `commit_once`. Do not copy a B0 future state into the edited branch. If `stage` rejects, record its exact reason and `STATE_ACTION_BLOCKED`; do not loosen stage. Only a successful commit permits a continuous edited-state split replay. Save predictions before GT scoring.
6. Build query-cutoff-only packets from true RGB and predicted masks, with separate local interaction and whole-fish appearance views. GT, future trajectory, public/native labels and stable color codes are withheld from image review. Unknown is valid. Codex inspection is not independent human relabeling.

The two oracles are causal decompositions, not candidate VLM methods. One successful exposed case would show existence only; two distinct interactions plus sustained state and input evidence are required before designing a paid association pilot. Even then this batch cannot report `METHOD PASS`.
